# Kwalify — beta tester guide

Thanks for trying Kwalify! This is a **closed beta**: playlists are generated from **your Spotify liked songs**.

**Site:** https://kwalify.net

---

## Before you start

Your Spotify account email must be on the app allowlist. If login fails with “not on allowlist”, ask the owner to add you in the Spotify Developer Dashboard.

---

## Steps

### 1. Connect Spotify
- Open https://kwalify.net  
- Click **Connect Spotify**  
- Approve permissions  

### 2. Sync your library
- After login, tap **Sync** (or wait for background sync)  
- First sync can take **1–5 minutes** for large libraries  
- Wait until sync finishes before generating  

### 3. Generate a playlist
- Describe a **moment**, not just a genre — e.g.  
  - *late-night motorway drive, mellow 2010s*  
  - *Sunday morning coffee, acoustic, calm*  
- Click generate — expect **30–90 seconds**  
- Open the playlist in Spotify when ready  

### 4. Tell us what you think
Please report:
- Did login work?  
- Did sync finish?  
- How long did generation take?  
- Was the playlist good for the prompt?  
- Phone or desktop?  

---

## Tips

- **Be specific** in prompts (place + energy + era)  
- **Discovery** mode surfaces less familiar tracks from your likes  
- If generation fails, wait a minute and try a slightly broader prompt  
- Hard refresh (Ctrl+F5) if buttons seem dead after an update  

---

## Privacy

Kwalify stores your liked-song metadata and generated playlists to power the app. See https://kwalify.net/privacy  

You can delete your data in **Settings** → delete account.
